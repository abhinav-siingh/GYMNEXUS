About GymNexus

Established in 2021, GymNexus has grown into one of Greater Noida's most trusted fitness clubs, built around a simple idea: consistent training, honest coaching, and a plan that fits your life — not the other way around. Founded by Abhinav Singh.

Every member gets access to a certified trainer, a diet plan tailored to their goals, and a club that's open early enough and late enough to fit around work and college.

Facilities

Everything you need for strength, recovery, and consistency, in one club.

🏋️

Free Weights & Strength Zone

A full range of barbells, dumbbells, kettlebells, and power racks for squats, deadlifts, and presses — built for serious strength training at every level, from first-timers to experienced lifters.

🏃

Cardio Zone

Treadmills, stationary cycles, and rowing machines with heart-rate tracking, laid out for both quick HIIT sessions and longer steady-state cardio.

💪

Certified Personal Trainers

One-on-one coaching from certified trainers who build your workout plan around your goals, experience level, and schedule, and adjust it as you progress.

🥗

Customized Diet Plans

Nutrition plans designed by your trainer and reviewed regularly — covering meal timing, macros, and portion sizes based on whether you're cutting, bulking, or maintaining.

🧘

Yoga & Mobility Studio

A dedicated, quieter space for yoga, stretching, and mobility work — ideal for warm-ups, injury-safe recovery, and improving flexibility alongside your strength training.

🚿

Locker & Shower Rooms

Clean, secure changing rooms with individual lockers and hot-water showers, so you can head straight to work or college after your session.

🕺

Group Classes & Zumba

High-energy group sessions — Zumba, HIIT, and functional training — for members who train better with music and a crowd instead of solo.

🧖

Steam Room & Recovery

A steam room and dedicated recovery corner for muscle relaxation and stiffness relief after heavy training days.
